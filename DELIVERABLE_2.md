In Delivery 1, each team defined how it will answer its research question and prepared an analysis-ready Zephyr dataset. For this delivery, your goal is to run the analysis on that dataset, answer the RQ and report the complete study in the final paper.

Only one group member needs to submit the deliverable. Each group will receive a grade based on peer evaluations collected after submission.

Deliverables
1. Final Report

Use the same Overleaf project and template from Delivery 1 and complete all sections that apply to your project. Submit the PDF generated from Overleaf.

Your Overleaf project now includes a template for the Results section, with guidelines and example figures. Use this template to write your results. The example figures use synthetic data, so replace them with figures of your own data or delete them.

The final report must:

revise the method sections from Delivery 1 (research design and data preparation) based on the Delivery 1 feedback
apply analysis methods to your final dataset and justify why each method fits your RQ and your data
report the results of the validation you planned in Delivery 1 (for example, human validation of automated detection or classification)
report results that answer each research question, using tables and figures.
Do not include Discussion or Threats to Validity sections. The paper for this delivery covers only your revised method and your results.

2. Replication Package

Extend your Delivery 1 replication package so that someone can reproduce your results, not only your dataset.

It must include:

the Delivery 1 code and data, updated if your pipeline changed.
code to run the analysis, producing every table and figure in the report.
any additional data your analysis uses, such as validation samples, coded samples or the codebook.
an updated README explaining exactly how to run the full pipeline from data retrieval to tables and figures.
The README should keep the Delivery 1 checkpoints and add the analysis checkpoints, for example:

Artifacts retrieved: N
After observation-period filter: N
After exclusions: N
Final observations: N
Validation sample: N
Observations used in the analysis: N
Delivery 2 ends with a research paper with your revised method and results and a replication package that reproduces both your dataset and your results.
